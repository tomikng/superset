## Self-host notes
- **No new required env vars.** `RELAY_URL` gained an upstream default (ms1 keeps its own), and `GITHUB_TOKEN`, `GOOGLE_SEARCH_CONSOLE_*`, `LEADERBOARD_INTERNAL_TOKEN` are optional. The R2 placeholders from 1.25.6 are already on ms1.
- **Relay:** upstream deleted its v1 relay and moved the Durable Objects relay to `apps/relay` (#7158), the same path as the fork's Bun port, so that directory now conflicts on every sync. Ours was kept wholesale and ported: `@superset/shared/tunnel-protocol` (renamed from `tunnel-v2-protocol`) and the `stream:dial-failed` fast path (502 "Host could not reach the relay"). `/health` still answers `proto: 2`.
- **i18n:** message ids are the English text now (#7092) and compiled catalogs are generated at build time, not committed (#7180). The fork's sign-in pages and leaderboard dropped their explicit ids; their translations were carried over. Mobile sign-in placeholders go through `t()` to satisfy upstream's hardcoded-string ratchet.
- Sign-in stays credential-only on desktop, web and mobile; Apple sign-in and the mobile social buttons remain removed.
- Backend: https://superset-api.tom-nguyen.dev · Update feed: https://superset-app.tom-nguyen.dev/releases
- Both the `.app` (inside the zip, used by the updater) and the DMG are signed, notarized and stapled.
